# Proposal sections, review criteria, and budgeting

## 1. Review criteria and where to answer them

| Common criterion | Answered in | What reviewers look for |
|---|---|---|
| Significance or importance | Background and significance | A real problem; consequences of solving it; why now |
| Innovation or novelty | Significance; approach | What is new compared with existing work, stated precisely |
| Approach or methodology | Methodology per aim | Feasible methods, sound design, analysis plan, risks and alternatives |
| Investigator capability | Team; preliminary work; biosketch | Track record, preliminary data, the right skills on the team |
| Environment | Facilities and resources | Access to equipment, data, participants, institutional support |
| Feasibility | Work plan; preliminary data | Timeline realistic for the budget and team |
| Impact and outcomes | Expected outcomes | Concrete outputs and who benefits |
| Value for money | Budget justification | Every cost tied to a task |

Read the funder's own criteria and their weights, and use their wording as headings where the format allows.

## 2. Specific aims page (one page)

| Paragraph | Content |
|---|---|
| Opening | The problem and why it matters, with one or two facts |
| Gap | What is missing that blocks progress |
| Long-term goal and objective | The programme's goal; this project's objective |
| Central hypothesis or approach | What will be tested or built, and the preliminary data behind it |
| Aims | Two to four, each with a one-line approach and expected outcome |
| Payoff | What will be possible when the aims are achieved |

Aims should be related but independent, so that a failure in one does not sink the others.

## 3. Aim structure

For each aim: rationale; hypothesis or objective; design; methods; analysis; expected results; potential problems and alternatives; milestones and success criteria.

## 4. Work plan

| Component | Detail |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Each tied to an aim |
| Milestones | Checkable events (dataset collected, model validated, paper submitted) |
| Deliverables | Publications, datasets, software, trained personnel, reports |
| Timeline | Gantt-style table by quarter or half-year |
| Responsibilities | Who leads each task |
| Dependencies | Which tasks wait for others |

## 5. Budget heads and justification

| Head | Typical contents | Justification pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Manpower | Research fellows, associates, assistants at the funder's rates | "One JRF for 36 months to carry out data collection and analysis for Aims 1 and 2." |
| Equipment (non-recurring) | Items with quotations | "A <item> is required for <task>; no equivalent is available in the department (letter attached)." |
| Consumables | Reagents, materials, cloud credits | Quantities times unit costs, linked to tasks |
| Travel | Fieldwork, conferences | Trips, purpose, cost basis |
| Contingency | As permitted | Funder's percentage |
| Other costs | Publication fees, participant payments, transcription, software licences | Itemised |
| Overheads | Institutional charge | Funder's permitted rate |

Year-wise table plus a line of justification for every item. Rates and permitted heads come from the funder's current guidelines.

## 6. Risk table

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation or alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recruitment slower than planned | medium | delays Aim 2 | Second site; extended window |
| Method fails on real data | low | Aim 1 output | Fallback method <...> |

## 7. Outcomes and impact

State numbers where realistic: papers, datasets, software releases, students trained, workshops, policy briefs, patents. Separate academic impact from societal or economic impact, and say who the beneficiaries are and how they will be reached.

## 8. Self-review as a panel

Score the draft 1 to 5 against each criterion, then:

| Score | Action |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Rewrite the section; this is where proposals fail |
| 3 | Strengthen with evidence, preliminary data, or a letter of support |
| 4–5 | Tighten wording only |

Common weaknesses: too many aims; methods described vaguely; no preliminary data; budget items not tied to tasks; timeline impossible; outcomes generic.

## 9. Compliance

Format (font, margins, page limits), required sections, attachments (endorsement, CVs, quotations, ethics clearances, letters), eligibility, and submission portal steps. Missing items cause rejection without review.
